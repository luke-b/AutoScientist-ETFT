## SLIDE 1 — The Eroom's Law Problem

**Headline:** *Science is getting exponentially more expensive — and slower.*

**Key visual:** Eroom's Law chart — R&D cost per new drug doubles every nine years
(mirror image of Moore's Law). Cross-reference: AI research paper volume doubles every
18 months, yet model architectures have converged. More compute, more papers, fewer
paradigm shifts.

**Speaker note / body copy:**

> The pharma industry spends $2.6 billion per approved drug. The ML community produces
> 400,000 papers per year but ships three genuinely new architectures per decade.
> The bottleneck is not compute — it is the human cognitive loop that decides *what to
> experiment on next*. Eroom's Law is not an industry anomaly. It is the default trajectory
> of any science driven by human intuition.

**Call to action:** *What if the scientific method itself could be automated?*

## SLIDE 2 — The $100B Blindspot in Every LLM

**Headline:** *Your AI doesn't know what it doesn't know.*

**Key visual:** Split diagram — left: LLM frozen weight space (static, dated); right:
arXiv preprints published after training cutoff (invisible to model).

**Body copy:**

Every frontier model — GPT, Claude, Gemini — was trained on data with a hard cutoff.
The instant training ends, the model's knowledge begins to decay relative to the frontier.

This is the **Knowledge Blindspot**: the gap between what a model believes is SOTA and
what actually is SOTA at inference time.

Current workarounds (RAG, fine-tuning) are **reactive patches**, not architectural solutions.
ETFT eliminates the blindspot structurally.

## SLIDE 3 — Introducing the Discrete Gradient

**Headline:** *Algorithms don't appear. They evolve.*

**Key visual:** Evolutionary tree — horizontal axis: time; vertical axis: fitness F(a).
Nodes: a₀ (MLP) → a₁ (1D CNN) → a₂ (Depthwise CNN) → a₃ (MobileNetV2) → a₄ (SOTA+1 ?)

Each transition labelled with the architectural delta (what changed) and the performance
jump (what it bought).

**Body copy:**

ETFT formalises this intuition mathematically.

Define a fitness function F(a) inversely proportional to validation loss under hard
hardware constraints. An **Evolutionary Trajectory** T = (a₀, a₁, …, aₙ) is an ordered
sequence where F(aᵢ₋₁) < F(aᵢ).

Each transition aᵢ₋₁ → aᵢ is a **macro-evolutionary jump** — a discrete gradient step
in architectural space. ETFT trains a model on these regressions, teaching it *the
mechanics of invention*, not just the outcomes.

> The model learns not what SOTA is. It learns *why* SOTA becomes SOTA.

## SLIDE 4 — The Regression Pipeline (Figure 1)

**Headline:** *Scalable training data — generated autonomously, validated rigorously.*

**Key visual:** Reproduction of Figure 1 from ETFT whitepaper.

```
  aₙ (Current SOTA)
       │
  AI Regression Agent (De-optimization)
       │
  CI/CD Validation ──── Fail (Syntax/OOM) → discarded
       │
       └── Pass (Functional) → aₙ₋₁ (Functional Predecessor)
                                    │
                            Extracted Meta-Datasets:
                            ├── D_Gen    (Generative Pairs)
                            ├── D_Rationale (Structural Meta-data)
                            └── D_Perf   (Performance Metrics)
```

**Body copy:**

The training corpus is generated by an **AI Regression Agent** that systematically
de-optimises SOTA algorithms into their functional predecessors — and records every
architectural decision along the way.

CI/CD validation ensures every generated predecessor is compilable and runnable.
The result: a self-growing, high-fidelity dataset of the evolutionary history of algorithms —
produced at scale, without human labelling.

**D_Perf** trains the **Probabilistic Heuristic Filter** — an in-silico triage that screens
out OOM-risk candidates before expensive GPU runs. Crucially, *failed* candidates
continuously enrich the negative training set, making the filter progressively sharper.

## SLIDE 5 — Pareto Delta Analysis: Finding the 20%

**Headline:** *Not all code is created equal. ETFT finds the 20% that drives 80% of progress.*

**Key visual:** Pareto bar chart — horizontal: architectural components (attention head,
normalization layer, activation function, quantization scheme…); vertical: contribution to
performance delta (aₙ₋₁ → aₙ). 80/20 cut-off line drawn.

**Body copy:**

Rather than real-time computational profiling (expensive, error-prone), ETFT performs
a **deterministic retrospective analysis** of historical deltas.

By comparing aₙ₋₁ vs aₙ across the entire evolutionary history, the system quantifies:
- Which sub-components were modified in winning transitions?
- What magnitude of change drove the largest performance jumps?

The model learns to recognise the structural signatures of high-yield innovation —
then projects that learned 80/20 lens onto the forward trajectory aₙ → aₙ₊₁.

This is not heuristic. It is a **deterministic, data-driven bottleneck oracle**.

## SLIDE 6 — The Open-Loop Architecture (Figure 2)

**Headline:** *A continuously self-improving R&D pipeline — running 24/7.*

**Key visual:** Reproduction of Figure 2 from ETFT whitepaper — the full Open-Loop
Empirical Ecosystem diagram with highlighted feedback loops.

```
  Current SOTA (aₙ)
       │
  Retrospective 80/20 Delta Analysis
       │
  Formulate Research Questions
       ├──────────────────────────────┐
  Literature Agents               Empirical Agents
  (Agentic RAG)                   (Micro-Experiments)
  Papers/Data ───────────────► Empirical Metrics
       └──────────────────────────────┘
                    │
          Augmented SOTA+1 Synthesis
                    │
          Probabilistic Filter (D_Perf Triage)
          ├── Pass (Viable) → Physical GPU Deployment ✓
          └── Fail (High-Value Error)
                    │
          Organic Negative Data
          └── In-Context RL Feedback → back to Empirical Agents
```

**Body copy:**

Once the 80/20 bottleneck is identified, two autonomous agent classes deploy in parallel:

1. **Literature Agents (Agentic RAG):** Scour arXiv and technical databases in real time —
   retrieving knowledge published *after* the model's training cutoff. Parametric blindspot
   eliminated.

2. **Empirical Agents:** Execute micro-experiments. Physical metrics flow back as RL reward
   signals. Failed experiments are *not discarded* — they become the highest-quality
   negative training examples the system can produce.

**The loop is self-hardening.** Every failure makes the next hypothesis smarter.
